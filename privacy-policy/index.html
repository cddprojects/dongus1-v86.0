Privacy policy

This page explains what the contact form on ScoutMerge does with a name, phone number, email address, and message.

This guide

ScoutMerge is an independent guide for job seekers in the United States. It is not an employer, it does not place workers, and the contact form is not a job application.

What the form collects

The contact form asks for:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • A message about work options

What happens after you send it

Your first name is kept in this browser tab so the thank-you page can greet you. This page does not save the phone number, email address, or message on a server. It does not sell personal information or share it with advertisers.

A reply inbox is not connected yet, so the form does not email the message to the guide.

Fonts

Pages load the Manrope typeface from Google Fonts. That request goes to Google and can include the browser’s IP address. The guide does not set its own advertising cookies.

Children

This guide is written for adult job seekers. It is not directed to children under 13.

Questions

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact form. The same limits above apply to that message.
